## Featured Projects
### [Lightweight Image-Space Deformation](https://tjj101.github.io/#lisd)
`C++` `GLSL` `OpenGL` `CMake` `ImGui`
 
A computer graphics research project on simulating surface bending, compression, and wrinkles in a fragment shader, **without modifying the underlying mesh**. It uses a height-guided parallax approach, with a pincer-style haptic input device in development.
 
- 📄 Research poster submitted to **Pacific Graphics 2026** ([read the poster](https://tjj101.github.io/media/lisd/A_Lightweight_Image_Space_Deformation_Method_PG26_Poster.pdf))
### [Get Me Out of Hell](https://tjj101.github.io/#get-me-out-of-hell)
`C++` `GLSL` `OpenGL ES 3.0` `Android Studio`
 
A creepy-cute 2D roguelike dungeon crawler inspired by Moon Rabbit folklore. Instead of exploring a pre-built dungeon, players **build their own dungeon one tile at a time**, alternating between map building and combat. Built on a custom engine for Windows and Android.
 
- 🏆 Selected for a public showcase at [**SIT Artistry 2026**](https://lnkd.in/p/g-N6imwh)
### [Dwarf Mayhem](https://tjj101.github.io/#dwarf-mayhem)
`C++` `Alpha Engine` `Cellular Automata`
 
A Noita-inspired 2D mining game. Players dig toward the core, sell materials for money, and buy upgrades while heat and limited storage push back. A **cellular-automata material simulation** drives liquids, solids, and gases that flow, sink, rise, and react with each other (water meets lava and makes stone and steam).
 
- 🎮 [Download the release](https://github.com/TJJ101/Dwarf-Mayhem-Into-the-Depths-release)
